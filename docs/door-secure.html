security and compliance

Keep it secure or compliant

One post-quantum engine, four insertion points. Greenfield systems adopt the native sealed wire; deployed systems that cannot change get the same protection grafted in, or simply inventoried.

The four modes

  • wrapAn outer RBX1 PQC-sealed envelope carried by the bus or a gateway. The bump-in-the-wire retrofit for crypto-less protocols (Modbus, CCSDS, financial rails).
  • kex-graftA hybrid or PQC key exchange grafted into the protocol's own handshake, no second cipher (TLS X25519MLKEM768, SSH, IKEv2 RFC 9370, SDLS, SRTP).
  • sig-graftSwap the signature to ML-DSA or SLH-DSA (FIPS 204/205) for firmware, PKI and DNSSEC surfaces.
  • observeRecognize on the wire and report crypto posture (the CISA/NSA cryptographic inventory), no carry, no keys.

Regimes and algorithms

  • CNSA 2.0 ML-KEM-1024 + ML-DSA-87, verified against liboqs and OpenSSL 3.6
  • FIPS 140-3 boundary via the validated-module sidecar
  • CSfC two-layer and NIST levels 1 to 5
