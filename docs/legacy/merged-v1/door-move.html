robotics and engineers

Move data between machines and robots

robobus is one shared-memory bus that every protocol plugs into, so a stream from one world reaches another without a bespoke gateway. The bridges are written for you.

What you get

  • 108 protocol adaptersROS 1, ROS 2, LSL, DDS, MQTT, Kafka, Zenoh, ZeroCM, serial, CAN and more, any-to-any on one bus.
  • Record and replay with no framework installedrosbag2, ROS 1 bag, XDF, MCAP (Foxglove) and EDF (BioSig), each verified against the real tool.
  • Visualize live in the browserTyped ROS 2 messages stream into Foxglove or the built-in 3D ground-control viewer.
  • Sealed on the wire and at restEvery route and recording can be post-quantum sealed with no code change.
